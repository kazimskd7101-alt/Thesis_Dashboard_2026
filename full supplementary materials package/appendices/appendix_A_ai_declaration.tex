\section*{Appendix A. Declaration of Generative AI Assistance}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix A. Declaration of Generative AI Assistance}
During the preparation of this thesis/manuscript package, generative AI tools, including ChatGPT, were used as auxiliary research-support instruments for organizing uploaded research materials, summarizing literature, improving academic wording, checking logical consistency, planning figures and tables, and supporting the interpretation and presentation of experimental outputs. The empirical data processing, experiment execution, result verification, final selection of arguments, and academic responsibility remain with the author.

AI assistance was not used as a substitute for empirical evidence. All numerical results reported in the manuscript must be traceable to the supplied datasets, result files, notebooks, images, dashboard files, or explicitly provided confirmed project facts. No citation, table, figure, or empirical claim should be included in the final version unless it is verified against the uploaded materials or an independently checked bibliographic source.

This appendix does not replace the HSE LMS AI-use declaration and survey. If AI was used in preparing the thesis, the author must declare it in the LMS upload form and complete the required HSE survey within the specified deadline.
